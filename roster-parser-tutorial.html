1. Quick Start Guide 5 steps

If you just want to get started right now, follow these 5 steps:

  1. Get your roster file — usually an Excel (.xlsx) file from your airline's portal or email.
  2. Open this tool — on your phone, tablet, or computer.
  3. Load the file — drag it onto the dotted area, or tap "Choose file" and select it.
  4. Review your schedule — the table appears automatically. Use the "Fit screen" button to see all columns.
  5. Save your roster — type your name, pick a timezone, and tap "Save roster" so you can later find free time with other crew.
💡 On a phone? Tap buttons with your finger — they are sized for touch. On a PC? Click with your mouse.

2. Loading Your Roster file upload

Where to get your roster file

Your airline usually provides a roster in one of these formats:

  • Excel file (.xlsx or .xls) — the best format. Often named "Roster Report".
  • PDF file — can be a "Roster Report" (table) or a "Calendar/Fridge" view.

Download the file to your device (email, company portal, etc.)

How to load the file

  1. On a computer: Drag the file from your folder onto the dotted area. Or click "Choose file" and pick it.
  2. On a phone/tablet: Tap "Choose file" — your device will ask you to pick a file from your storage (Downloads, Photos, Files).
  3. After selecting, the file starts processing automatically. You'll see a message like XLSX or PDF · Roster Report.

If the format is not recognised correctly

Sometimes the tool might guess the wrong PDF type. If you see a "Not right? force re-parse as:" section, try the other button:

  • "Roster Report table" — use this if your PDF looks like a spreadsheet with rows and columns.
  • "Calendar / fridge" — use this if your PDF looks like a monthly calendar grid.
⚠️ PDF parsing can be imperfect — if you can, always use the .xlsx version for best results.

4. Toolbar Buttons Explained actions

These buttons appear above your roster. Each does a specific job:

Fit screen
What: Scales the table so all columns fit your screen width.
When to use: If the table is too wide and you have to scroll sideways. Tap it once to fit; tap again to go back to normal size.
Copy
What: Copies the entire roster (including headers) to your device's clipboard.
When to use: Paste it into a text message, email, or spreadsheet app.
CSV
What: Downloads a .csv file that can be opened in Excel, Numbers, or Google Sheets.
When to use: For further analysis or printing in a spreadsheet.
Image
What: Saves a high‑quality image (PNG) of your roster.
When to use: To share your schedule or keep a picture on your phone.
On iPhone: After tapping, you'll see a share sheet — tap "Save Image" to save it to your Photos.
On Android: A share sheet opens — choose "Save to Downloads" or "Save to Gallery". Cancelling share sheet will auto download the image to Downloads folder.
On PC: The image downloads directly to your Downloads folder.
Print / PDF
What: Opens your browser's print dialog.
When to use: To get a clean paper printout or to save as a PDF file.
How to save as PDF: In the print dialog, choose "Save as PDF" instead of a real printer.
Important: Set the orientation to Portrait (vertical) for best results.
💡 On mobile, the "Print / PDF" button may use your phone's print service (e.g., AirPrint on iOS). You can also choose "Save as PDF" there.

5. Editing the Code Legend customize

The legend shows what each duty code means. You can change or add descriptions:

  1. Edit a description — tap or click on any description field (the right‑hand box). Type your own text.
  2. Add a new code — tap the "+ add code" button. A new row appears. Enter the code (e.g., STBY) and its description.
  3. Delete a code — tap the "✕" button next to the row you want to remove.
  4. Changes are saved automatically — your customisations stay on this device, even after reloading the page.
⚠️ The legend is stored in your browser's local storage. If you clear your browser history/cache, your changes will be lost. Consider this when you switch devices.

6. Saving Rosters for later use

You must save your roster if you want to use the "Find Time Together" feature. Saving also lets you keep a copy of your schedule.

How to save

  1. After your roster appears, scroll down to the "Save This Roster" section.
  2. Enter your name — any name you like (e.g., "Ikhlas", "Nadiya", or your full name).
  3. Choose a timezone:
    • SLT — your home base time (KUL / UTC+8). This is the default.
    • UTC — Coordinated Universal Time. Use this if you prefer to see everything in UTC.
  4. Tap "Save roster". You'll see a confirmation message.

Managing your saved rosters

Below the save section, you'll see a list of all saved rosters. You can:

  • View — click the View button to reload that roster and see it on screen.
  • Delete — click the Delete button to remove it (this can't be undone).
  • Select for overlap — check the checkbox next to a roster to include it in "Find Time Together".
⚠️ Saved rosters are stored only on this browser. If you use a different phone/computer, you'll need to save the roster again on that device.

7. Finding Time Together 💞 crew sync

This is the main reason to save rosters — it finds overlapping free time between 2 or more crew members.

Step‑by‑step

  1. Save at least 2 rosters with different names (e.g., "Ikhlas" and "Nadiya").
  2. In the "Find Time 2B Together" section, check the boxes next to the crew members you want to coordinate.
  3. Tap "Find overlap". The tool will analyse all selected rosters.
  4. Results appear:
    • If you selected 3 or more, you'll see "All {N} together" — time when everyone is free.
    • Below that, you'll see pairwise breakdowns — overlap between each pair (e.g., Ikhlas × Nadiya).
    • Each result shows the date, time, location, duration, and whether it's Confirmed or Maybe (standby‑dependent).
  5. You can also tap "Heatmap view" to see a calendar with coloured bars — more visual.

How the tool decides what's "free"

  • Off days (D, DO, EL) — completely free (confirmed).
  • Layovers (LO) — free time away from base (confirmed).
  • Gaps between duties — any time between report and debrief that isn't flying is counted as free.
  • Standby duties — marked as Maybe because you might be called in.
💡 If two people are in different locations (e.g., one in KUL, one in BKK), the tool only counts overlaps when they are in the same station.

8. Heatmap View visual calendar

After running "Find overlap", tap "Heatmap view" to see a calendar‑like grid.

  • Each day is a small box.
  • The box is filled with a horizontal bar that shows free time from midnight (left) to midnight (right).
  • Green = time everyone is free together (confirmed).
  • Gold = time that depends on standby (maybe).
  • Red = not free.
  • Tap any box to see a popup that tells you who has to leave first for their next duty — useful if you're planning something and need to know when to wrap up.

To go back to the table, tap "Table view".

💡 The heatmap is especially handy when comparing multiple people's free time and during gathering indicate who has to leave if its not a full day off together.

9. Statistics Panel monthly summary

Automatically shown below the roster table. It gives you a quick summary of your month:

Actual Block Hours
Total flight hours for the month.
Duty Hours
Total duty hours (from report to debrief).
OFF Days At Base
Number of days you were off and at your home base (KUL).
OFF Days Away From Base
Number of days you were off but away from base — includes layovers (LO).
💡 "OFF Days Away From Base" counts any day you're not flying but also not at home base.

10. Troubleshooting help

where to get my Excel .xlsx file?

  1. Log in to your Company's Roster Website "Download Excel"
  2. use the XLSX version if available — it's much more reliable.
  3. If something goes wrong, click on "Export debug data" (near the bottom) and send that file to Zayn Ikhlas' Dad.

"Find Time Together" shows no results

  • The selected rosters might not have overlapping date ranges.Make sure the roster are from the same month
  • They might be at different stations (e.g., one in KUL, one in BKK) during the entire overlap.
  • Check that you have saved both rosters with the correct timezone setting(SLT).

My custom legend disappeared

⚠️ Legend changes are stored in your browser's local storage. If you clear browsing data (cache, cookies, site data), they'll be lost. To avoid this, don't clear site data for this page.

General tips

  • All processing is 100% offline — nothing is uploaded anywhere. Your privacy is protected.
  • If something looks wrong, refresh the page and try again.
  • For best results, use the latest version of Chrome, Safari, or Firefox.
