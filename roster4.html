📱 Badge guide:
• 🟢 Rest (light green) – off duty at home (no alarm).
• 🟢 REST in XXX (dark green) – off duty, but on a layover away from base (no alarm).
• ⏰ Rest until HH:MM (yellow) – duty report time later today (4h alarm).
• 🔴 N/S \| Rest until HH:MM (red) – layover day with a duty report time (4h alarm).
• 🛫 DEP XXX HH:MM – flight departure today (4h alarm).
• 🛬 ARR XXX HH:MM GO rest! – flight arrival today (4h alarm).
• 🔴 N/S in XXX \| Rest until HH:MM (red) – extra layover row between legs (4h alarm).
• ❌ No alarms on D, DO, DO1, DO2 (Rest days).
