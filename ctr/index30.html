Quick Guide

  • 1. Upload PDF (CrewTripReport) → parse crew data.
  • 2. Upload Gendec .xlsx → names are taken from the gendec as the source of truth.
  • 3. If some staff IDs still can't be matched, upload more gendecs — or click Continue Anyway to proceed with CrewTripReport names for those crew (they'll be marked ⚠).
  • 4. Select destination → click "Generate Forms" → filter modal appears.
  • 5. Choose which crew members appear in each template, then click Generate.
  • 6. Fine-tune positions with the compact controls, print or download.
