Quick Guide

  • 1. Upload PDF (CrewTripReport) → parse crew data.
  • 2. Upload Gendec .xlsx → auto‑parsed and merged.
  • 3. Select destination → click "Generate Forms" → filter modal appears.
  • 4. Choose which crew members appear in each template, then click Generate.
  • 5. Fine‑tune positions with the compact controls, print or download.
