Quick Guide

  • 1. Upload a PDF (or paste its text) → click "Parse PDF Report"
  • 2. Select your destination (e.g., BOM) from the dropdown
  • 3. Click "Generate Forms" – all templates for that destination will load
  • 4. Fine‑tune each form individually using its sliders or type values directly
  • 5. Print each form separately, or download all as PNG/PDF
